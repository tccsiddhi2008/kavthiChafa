Kavti Chapha

Botanical Name: Plumeria alba (Magnolia Grandiflora)

Common Name: Kavthi-Chafa

Family: Magnoliaceae

Description: A small tree with thick, glossy green leaves and fragrant white flowers.

Medicinal Uses:

1. Traditionally used in herbal preparations for skin problems and inflammation.
2. Used traditionally to help with inflammation and pain.
3. Traditionally used for fever.
